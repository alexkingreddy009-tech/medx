# MEDX 🩺
### An AI-powered Medical Learning & Clinical Intelligence Platform

**MEDX** is an open-source project aimed at transforming how medical students, interns, and doctors learn, revise, and access clinical knowledge.

The vision is to bring medical education, exam preparation, clinical reasoning, diagnostic learning, and medical knowledge management together in one intelligent platform.

## 🎯 Vision

Build an accessible, privacy-conscious Medical Learning OS that helps healthcare professionals learn continuously and make better-informed clinical decisions.

## ✨ Planned Features

- **Clinical Learning:** Structured learning resources and clinical case discussions.
- **Exam Preparation:** MCQs, explanations, revision systems, and progress tracking.
- **Diagnostic Reasoning:** Interactive clinical scenarios and differential diagnosis practice.
- **Medical Knowledge Base:** Organised medical notes, references, and learning resources.
- **Medical Updates:** Access to relevant medical guidelines and developments.
- **Procedural Learning:** Educational resources for clinical procedures and practical skills.
- **AI Integration:** AI-powered assistance using both local and cloud-based models.

## 🧠 Technology Philosophy

- Local-first AI wherever practical.
- Privacy-conscious architecture.
- Open-source development.
- Modular and extensible design.
- Accessible to medical learners globally.
